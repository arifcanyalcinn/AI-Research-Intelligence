| TD-018 | The recency signal is structurally ~0 for `huggingface_models` and `huggingface_spaces`, and topic relevance is near-zero for them too. Confirmed by arithmetic on live data — `huggingface_spaces` mean 0.294 against 0.2875 predicted from authority (`0.25 × 0.45`) and an expected engagement percentile of 0.5 (`0.35 × 0.5`) alone, leaving ≤0.0065 for recency and topic combined, which also bounds mean topic relevance at ≤0.043. The topic half follows from TD-003: both sources have `abstract=None`, so topic matching sees only title and tags. Authority also places these two lowest (0.55, 0.45), so three of the four signals push the same two sources in the same direction | Sourcing `lastModified` does **not** fix it. The effective options are: lower `ranking.recency_k`, accepting that it flattens recency for every source; change the fetch strategy to `sort=lastModified` or trending, which collides with TD-005 (sort key hardcoded, SDS amendment required); or accept that all-time-popularity sources are structurally down-weighted.
