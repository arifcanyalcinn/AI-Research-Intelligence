| TD-018 | The recency signal is structurally ~0 for `huggingface_models` and `huggingface_spaces`, and topic relevance is near-zero for them too. Both fetch by all-time popularity (`sort=downloads` / `sort=likes`) and take `published_date` from `createdAt`, so their items are typically years old: `exp(-0.15 × 700) ≈ 0`. Confirmed by arithmetic on live data — `huggingface_spaces` mean 0.294 against 0.2875 predicted from authority (`0.25 × 0.45`) and an expected engagement percentile of 0.5 (`0.35 × 0.5`) alone, leaving ≤0.0065 for recency and topic combined, which also bounds mean topic relevance at ≤0.043. Most FILTERED items come from this source | Correct per SDS §5.5, which defines recency as `exp(-k × days_elapsed)` over `published_date` and says nothing about activity. The three sources that score well (`huggingface_papers`, `arxiv`, `github_trending`) do so because their fetch strategies select recent items by construction, not because the signal distinguishes them. The topic half follows from TD-003: both sources have `abstract=None`, so topic matching sees only title and tags. Authority also places these two lowest (0.55, 0.45), so three of the four signals push the same two sources in the same direction | Sourcing `lastModified` does **not** fix it. Verified against the live API: `lastModified` is absent from the default list response, so it is not in the stored raw payloads either; retrieving it needs `expand[]=lastModified`, which *replaces* the default field set and silently drops `sdk`, `tags` and `createdAt` unless every field is enumerated. And even with it, the top-liked Spaces were last modified 210 and 100 days ago — still `exp(-0.15 × 100) ≈ 0`. The effective options are: lower `ranking.recency_k`, accepting that it flattens recency for every source; change the fetch strategy to `sort=lastModified` or trending, which collides with TD-005 (sort key hardcoded, SDS amendment required); or accept that all-time-popularity sources are structurally down-weighted. Decide before tuning `min_score`, since the two interact. **Confirmed in production, Batch 9:** a sampled `huggingface_models` row carries `"recency": 5.67e-33` in its `signal_breakdown` — `exp(-0.15 × ~500 days)` — so the signal contributes nothing measurable for that source. The prediction is now an observation, and the arithmetic above is confirmed by a stored per-item value rather than inferred from a source mean |
